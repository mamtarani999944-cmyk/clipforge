Our story

ClipForge started as a simple tool to save time cutting long-form video into short clips. It's built and maintained by an independent developer, with a focus on making short-form content creation faster for creators everywhere.

We're constantly shipping improvements — from smarter scene detection to better captioning and AI-powered scoring — with more on the way.
